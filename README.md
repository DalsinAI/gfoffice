# GFOffice

## A modern office suite, made for the Amiga

Write a letter, balance the books, give a talk, lay out a newsletter, keep a
database, plan a project or sketch a diagram, all on your Amiga.

GFOffice is seven native Amiga applications that open the documents people
send you today, let you work on them properly, and save them so anyone can
read them again. It is free and open source.

![GFOpenWrite on AmigaOS 3.2.3, showing a DOCX document as it was written](docs/screenshot.png)

## What's inside

- **GFOpenWrite** for letters, reports and long documents.
- **GFOpenCalc** for spreadsheets, with formulas, charts and pivot tables.
- **GFOpenPresentation** for slides and full-screen slide shows.
- **GFOpenPressmaster** for newsletters, flyers and booklets.
- **GFOpenBase** for databases, with forms, queries and reports.
- **GFOpenProject** for project plans and Gantt charts.
- **GFOpenDiag** for flowcharts, diagrams and mind maps.

![GFOpenPresentation's slide sorter with a deck open](docs/screenshots/gfopenpresentation.png)

## Why GFOffice

- **Works with today's files.** Microsoft Office and OpenDocument files open
  and save, and every application makes PDF.
- **Feels like home.** Real Amiga windows, menus and gadgets, and ARexx in
  every application.
- **Light on hardware.** A 68040 with 8 MB of Fast RAM is enough. No FPU
  needed.
- **Free and open.** MIT licensed, and written from scratch.

## Get it

1. Download the latest release from
   [Releases](https://github.com/DalsinAI/gfoffice/releases).
2. Unpack it to a drawer such as `Work:GFOffice`.
3. Double-click an application, or drop a document on it.

You need AmigaOS 3.2 or later and a 68040.
